Echostar Training Program

Role: Learning Experience Designer ❧ 2026

Designed and developed a comprehensive digital training curriculum for call center agents. Utilizing Articulate Rise, HTML, CSS, and AI-assisted tools like Gemini, I built four digital instructor-led training (ILT) courses per month.

[ Screenshot of Articulate Rise Module ]

A major focus of this project was accessibility and innovation. I created a responsive, accessible survey embed template that allowed users to open surveys in new tabs, successfully bypassing a software error that hindered previous methods. All modules were rigorously audited for WCAG compliance.

House Rules Platform

Role: Product Designer ❧ 2026

An independent project dedicated to digitizing paper-based party games while preserving the unique "house rules" and inside jokes of individual friend groups.

I leveraged AI models, specifically Claude, to conduct thematic analysis on exploratory user research regarding social gaming behaviors. This accelerated the development of a highly personalized, user-centered interface.
