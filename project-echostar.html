How To: Lead Training
For 2,000+ Agents.

Current Role
Learning Experience Designer & Dev
Audience

The Big Picture

Looking at pictures of software doesn’t teach you how to use it.

Standard training tools trap 2,000+ agents in passive slides, leading to real call mistakes. In my role at Echostar, I build lightweight interactive sandboxes, custom mini-games, and clean web templates that give agents safe, hands-on practice before their first live call.

Three Core Solutions

Select a tab or use your arrow keys to explore the work.

Playable Sandboxes & Accessible Games

Articulate Rise only supports static images and videos. To train agents on the complex, legacy DISH Business portal, I reverse-engineered the app’s code and built safe, clickable HTML/JS sandboxes right inside their lessons.

Practice Sandboxes

Agents type and navigate without fear of breaking customer accounts. Classroom feedback: "I wish we could've had this the first time!"

Accessible Games

Call simulations and dress-up knowledge checks built with full keyboard navigation, alt tags, and high-contrast color schemes.

Fixing the Flawed Workaround in 6 Hours

My team knew our internal survey software update would block traditional course embeds. Leadership drafted a workaround, but it relied on messy, confusing code that was bound to break for our non-technical designers on launch day.

8:00 AM

The Flaw: Recognized that the proposed workaround was too brittle and confusing for teammates to maintain under a 4:00 PM deadline.

10:00 AM

The Clean Fix: Arrived early and coded a clean, modular pop-up block using HTML/JS and Gemini that reliably talks to Rise’s completion gates.

1:00 PM

Documentation: Secured leadership approval and shared an easy, copy-paste guide (SOP) with plain-language comments for the team.

4:00 PM

On-Time Launch: The team launched across multiple lines of business on schedule with 100% of compliance records preserved.

Human-in-the-Loop Leadership

Code Literacy

AI Prompt Guardrails
